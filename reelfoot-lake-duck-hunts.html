Why the lake

A Tennessee Duck Hunting Lake With Real Tradition

Reelfoot is not a generic hunting destination. It has its own water, its own cover, and its own way of hunting.

Cypress brakes, changing weather, birds moving through the Mississippi River Flyway, and a lake that has drawn hunters to Northwest Tennessee for generations.

A guided hunt with Parker's gives your group a clear way to hunt Reelfoot without trying to figure the lake out on your own. You bring the group. Parker's handles the blind, the guide work, and the details that make the day run right.

Legal shooting time on the lake closes at 3pm daily. Parker's runs its own dogs and is not equipped to accommodate customers bringing hunting dogs, on the lake or on private ground.
